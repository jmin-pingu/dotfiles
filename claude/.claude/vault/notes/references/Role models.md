# Role models

People and blogs whose way of learning and explaining I want to copy.
Claude uses this list to shape how it teaches me (see `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, Teaching mode).

## How to fill this in

For each person, the most useful column is **"How they learn"**: the habit to copy, not just the topic.
Examples of habits: derives things from first principles, builds it from scratch to understand it, writes to learn, uses lots of small worked examples, reads the original paper.
